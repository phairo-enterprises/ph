Comma Local Remote

One local session. The Comma touchscreen authorizes devices and every settings change.

Waiting

Verification

After the Comma touchscreen approves this device, wait 30 seconds without sending commands. Then choose the six-digit number displayed on the Comma.

Choosing the wrong number permanently blocks this browser ID until device-side unblocking.

Settings proposals

Changes are not applied until Confirm Changes is selected on the Comma itself.
